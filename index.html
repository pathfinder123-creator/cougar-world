    Navigate the Workplace

    Slow the situation down, consider what may be happening, and choose one constructive next action. This tool will not diagnose your workplace or decide for you.

    Step 1 of 4About 5–10 minutes

    Name the situation

    What are you navigating?

    Choose the situation closest to what you need today. You can return later for something different.

    Consider before concluding

    More than one explanation may be true. Choose the lens that seems most useful to examine first.

    What do I know?

    Focus on words, actions, dates, expectations, and outcomes you could describe without interpreting motive.

    What might I be assuming?

    Notice the meaning or intention you have attached to the facts. An assumption may be reasonable—but it may still need checking.

    Some concerns deserve additional support

    If this involves threats, immediate danger, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or a serious safety concern, a reflective tool is not a substitute for qualified help. Review your employer’s reporting options and consider authoritative resources from the EEOC ↗ or OSHA ↗. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

    Choose what matters
